BEFORE YOU ORDER ♡

Order & Shop Information

A few little things to know before your handmade order reaches you 🧶
🧶

Processing Time

Every item is handmade with care. Processing time may vary depending on the product, quantity and customisation requested.

📦

Shipping & Delivery

Delivery charges and estimated delivery time will be confirmed while your order is being finalised through WhatsApp.

💳

Payment

Payment details will be shared after your order and availability are confirmed. Please complete payment only through the details provided by us.

♡

Cancellations & Returns

Since our products are handmade, especially custom orders, cancellations or returns may be limited once work has started. Please contact us if there is an issue with your order.

🔒

Your Privacy

The contact and delivery details you provide are used only for communicating about and fulfilling your order. Please avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information.
